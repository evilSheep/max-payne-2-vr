Gore (wounds, blood and dismemberment) - third-party assets
============================================================

The wound and dismemberment system (maxpayne2gore.asi) draws an inner skeleton built from
the models below. Both are compiled into the mod in modified form: reduced to a lower
polygon count and refitted to Max Payne 2's character skeleton. The original model files
are not included in the download or the repository.

"Low Poly Skeleton For Maya" by 606tom
  https://www.renderhub.com/606tom/lowpoly-skeleton
  Licence: RenderHub Extended Use License (https://renderhub.com/info/3d-content-licensing).
  Used only as part of this mod; it may not be extracted or redistributed on its own.
  Use: the skeleton's body (spine, rib cage, pelvis, limbs, hands and feet). Modified:
  polygons reduced, its skull removed, bones refitted.

"Human Skull" by CDmir
  https://opengameart.org/content/human-skull-0
  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain) - https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Use: the skull, jaw and teeth. Modified: reduced from 4,548 to about 1,500 triangles,
  fitted to each character's head.

The skull and muscle textures were made for this project by evilSheep.

No Max Payne 2 game files are included. The muscle layer and every other game-derived
shape are built at run time from the player's own installation.
